### 📂 Featured Projects

#### 1. [DELA — Bank Statement Extraction Engine](https://github.com/abdilahi99official)
* **Problem**: Tanzanian accounting and finance professionals waste hours manually transcribing bank statement PDFs into Excel spreadsheets for audit and reconciliation.
* **Solution**: A privacy-first conversion service that extracts, normalizes, and mathematically validates transaction data from PDF bank statements, outputting structured `.xlsx` workbooks.
* **Key Implementation**: Built with FastAPI and Pydantic for deterministic balance validation; containerized with Docker on Railway with automated background jobs to purge temporary files post-conversion.
* **Stack**: `Python` • `FastAPI` • `Pydantic` • `pdfplumber` • `openpyxl` • `Docker` • `Railway`

#### 2. [AgriPride Market Link — Agricultural Price Intelligence](https://github.com/abdilahi99official/AgriPride-Market-Link)
* **Problem**: Smallholder farmers in rural Tanzania lack access to real-time market price data due to poor internet connectivity and heavy web interfaces.
* **Solution**: A text-first, low-bandwidth web platform that delivers grain market pricing and trend insights optimized for 2G/3G network conditions.
* **Key Implementation**: Built on Next.js Server-Side Rendering (SSR) to keep client payloads under 50KB, secured via Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) for Market Officers, and integrated with `pgvector` and Vercel AI SDK for grounded price trend queries.
* **Stack**: `Next.js` • `TypeScript` • `Tailwind CSS` • `Supabase` • `PostgreSQL` • `Vercel AI SDK` • `Vercel`
